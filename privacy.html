Privacy Policy

Pawsulin · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Pawsulin is a logbook for people caring for a diabetic dog or cat. We built it so your pet's health information stays with you.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — pet profiles, glucose readings, insulin shots, meals, events, notes and settings — is stored only on your phone. We do not have servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it. There is no account and no sign-in.

Backups and exports

Pawsulin keeps automatic backup copies on your phone. When you export a backup, CSV or PDF report, the file goes only where you choose to send it (for example email, Files or Google Drive). Those services handle the file under their own privacy policies.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system. No reminder data leaves your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information needed to validate purchases. It does not receive your pet's health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

No ads. No advertising or tracking SDKs. No selling or sharing of data. No analytics on your health records.

Children

Pawsulin is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, deleting the app deletes it. You can also delete individual records or pets inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at the support email shown on Pawsulin's App Store or Google Play page.
